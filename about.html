About Atul Sah

Profile & Philosophy

I am a Computer Science Educator, Author of 8+ technical books, and Full-Stack Developer based in Patna, India. Currently, I serve as a Faculty member in the Department of Computer Application at B. N. College (Patna University), instructing undergraduate students and guiding technical research projects.

I completed both my Master of Computer Applications (MCA) and Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) from Patna University, building a strong theoretical and algorithmic foundation in database architectures, distributed systems, and software engineering.

My professional identity bridges Software Engineering and Academic Instruction. Teaching forces me to understand technologies down to their fundamental mathematical and architectural layers, while building production systems ensures that my students and readers receive industry-tested, contemporary knowledge.

As a published author of eight technical books distributed on Amazon, Google Books, and Pothi, I specialize in translating complex computer science paradigms into structured learning resources spanning Agentic AI, Java, Machine Learning, and Cybersecurity.
